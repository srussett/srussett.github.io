Climate Dynamics & Paleoclimate

Evaluating Paleoclimate Reconstruction Skill in East Africa

Manuscript in progress

Assessing how well available paleoclimate reconstruction products capture East African rainfall variability. My results highlight significant regional and global differences between leading DA products, with implications for future development of paleoclimate reconstructions in proxy-sparse regions and their applications for climate insights.

Weird Winter: Anomalous North American Circulation, DJFM 2025–26

Manuscript in progress

An ERA5-based look at the anomalous 2025-6 winter in the western United States. I aim to understand why the season resulted in such low snowpacks in the western US, and characterize the conditions under which the anomalous features occurred.

Multidecadal Variability in East African Hydroclimate

Analysis in progress

Research on the variability of East Africa's long and short rainy seasons, including their teleconnections to El Niño–Southern Oscillation and the Indian Ocean Dipole.
