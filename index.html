Climate & Paleoclimate Science

PhD Student · Earth and Environmental Sciences · Columbia University

I am a Ph.D. candidate studying Earth's paleoclimate! My research focuses on a couple of key questions:

  1. Are the tools that we use to reconstruct Earth's past climate over the past few centuries any good? How well do they work in areas with fewer proxy records available?
  2. Can we understand the decadal variability of rain and drought in the past, and how it is connected to ocean-atmosphere cycles like ENSO and the Indian Ocean Dipole?
  3. How have humans experienced changes in hydroclimate across history?
