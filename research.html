Multidecadal Variability in East African Hydroclimate

QC 929 .D76 R87
Columbia University · Graduate Research, 2024–present

Independent research on the drivers of East Africa's long and short rainy seasons, including their teleconnections to El Niño–Southern Oscillation and the Indian Ocean Dipole. Methods include low-pass, high-pass, and Butterworth filtering, wavelet coherence, red-noise significance testing, and lead/lag correlation mapping.

Resolving Drought Drivers in East African Hydroclimate

QC 981.8 .C5 R87
Presented at AGU Fall Meeting 2025 & Gordon Research Conference on Climate 2025

Work presented as "Resolving Drought Drivers and Multidecadal Variability in East African Hydroclimate" (AGU 2025) and "The Dynamics of Drought in East Africa" (GRC 2025), examining the dynamic cycles underlying drought risk in the region.

Evaluating Paleoclimate Reconstruction Skill in the Bimodal Rains Region

QE 501.4 .P34 R87
Columbia University · Graduate Research, 2024–present

Assessing how well available paleoclimate reconstruction products capture East African rainfall variability, by comparing reconstruction performance against 20th-century instrumental records in the bimodal rains region.

WeirdWinter: Anomalous North American Circulation, DJFM 2025–26

QC 995 .N67 R87
In progress · ERA5-based side project

An ERA5-based look at the unusual winter circulation pattern over North America during the 2025–26 season — tracking the anomaly's evolution through the winter and situating it against the historical record of comparably weird winters.
